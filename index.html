Flagship case study · Shipped AI product

KnewSooner: an AI guide to Canadian money for newcomers

Newcomers to Canada have to open a bank account, build credit from zero, and understand taxes and benefits, usually in their second language. I own the vision, roadmap and requirements for the Finance section and its AI co-pilot.

What I did

Discovery & scope

Researched competitors and newcomer questions, then structured the section around what people need first: ID requirements, account setup, fees, building credit, taxes, benefits and pay.

Evals before scale

Directed data scientists to build gold datasets and rubrics scoring answers for accuracy, relevance and safety on banking, credit and tax questions.

Delivery & pricing

Ran sprint planning, technical requirements and API specs; led pricing work aligned to customer needs and competitors.

Results

12 wksTo first release

When not to trust the model

Money questions have real consequences for someone new to the system. Answers about tax obligations, credit products and fees are checked against the rubric, cite their source, and point to the official program or bank page instead of improvising when the model's confidence is low.
